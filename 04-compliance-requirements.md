# 04 - Compliance Requirements

## Requirement 1: Clear Notice

The system should provide students with clear information about:

- What personal data is collected.
- Why it is collected.
- How it is used.
- How students can exercise applicable rights.
- How students can raise complaints.

## Requirement 2: Lawful Processing

The college should identify the applicable legal basis for each processing activity and document it.

## Requirement 3: Consent

Where processing relies on consent, the college should provide an appropriate mechanism for obtaining and managing consent.

## Requirement 4: Withdrawal of Consent

Where consent is the applicable basis, students should have an accessible method to withdraw consent.

## Requirement 5: Correction and Erasure

The system should provide appropriate mechanisms for students to request correction or erasure where applicable.

## Requirement 6: Grievance Redressal

The college should provide a mechanism through which students can submit privacy-related complaints.

## Requirement 7: Security Safeguards

The college should implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data.

## Requirement 8: Data Retention

The college should establish retention and deletion procedures consistent with applicable requirements.

## Requirement 9: Third-Party Processing

Where external vendors process personal data, the college should establish appropriate contractual and operational controls.

## Requirement 10: Personal Data Breach

The college should establish a procedure for detecting, responding to, documenting, and handling personal data breaches according to applicable requirements.

## Requirement 11: Accountability

The college should maintain appropriate documentation showing how privacy and data protection requirements are being implemented.
