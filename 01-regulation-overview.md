# 01 - Regulation Overview

## 1. Regulation

The regulation selected for this project is the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 provide additional requirements and implementation details.

## 2. Purpose of the Regulation

The regulation establishes a framework for processing digital personal data while recognizing individuals' rights relating to their personal data.

## 3. Important Concepts

### Data Principal

The individual to whom the personal data relates.

For this project, a student whose information is processed by the library system is an example of a Data Principal.

### Data Fiduciary

The person or organization that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.

For this example, the college may act as the Data Fiduciary for the library system.

### Personal Data

Information relating to an individual who can be identified from that information.

Examples in the library system include:

- Student name
- Student ID
- Email address
- Phone number
- Borrowing history

## 4. Compliance Objective

The college should identify its obligations under the applicable law and implement appropriate processes, technical controls, and organizational measures.
