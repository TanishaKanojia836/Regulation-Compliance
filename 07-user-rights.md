# 07 - User Rights

## 1. Student Rights

The college should provide appropriate mechanisms for students to exercise rights available to them under the applicable law.

## 2. Correction

Students should have a process for requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

### Example

If a student's email address is incorrect, the student should be able to request its correction.

---

## 3. Erasure

Where applicable, students may request erasure of personal data, subject to circumstances where retention is required by law or otherwise permitted/required.

---

## 4. Grievance Redressal

Students should have a clearly communicated way to raise privacy-related complaints.

---

## 5. Nomination

The system should support the applicable nomination requirements established by the law and rules.

---
